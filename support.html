《蛇宝开饭啦》帮助与支持

用蛇身把会动脑子的小动物围住吃掉——蛇身就是围栏。

怎么玩

  • 滑动屏幕转向；手指按住不动开始潜行，松手恢复正常速度。
  • 正面撞上能吃，但用蛇身绕一圈把动物围住更值钱：一圈 N 只 = 分数 ×(N+1)。
  • 每种动物有自己的逃法：老鼠钻洞、青蛙跳过一层蛇身、兔子快、鸡会叫来农夫、小鸟见你冲过来就飞。
  • 一局 4 分钟，动物会越来越机灵。

常见问题

需要联网吗？ 不需要，游戏完全离线运行，飞行模式也能玩。

有内购或广告吗？ 没有。

支持哪些语言？ 简体中文、繁体中文、英语、日语、韩语、西班牙语、葡萄牙语、法语、德语，跟随系统语言自动切换。

怎么关音效？ 游戏中点右上角 ⏸ 暂停，在暂停页开关音效。

Snack Snake: Help & Support

Trap clever little animals by wrapping your snake around them: your body is the fence.

How to play

  • Swipe to turn. Keep a finger resting on the screen to sneak; lift it to go back to normal speed.
  • You can bite animals head-on, but looping your body around them pays far more: N animals in one ring = score ×(N+1).
  • Each animal has its own escape: mice dive into holes, frogs hop over one layer of snake, rabbits are fast, chickens call the farmer, and birds fly off if you rush them.
  • A round lasts 4 minutes, and the animals get smarter as it goes on.

FAQ

Do I need an internet connection? No. The game runs fully offline, even in airplane mode.

Are there ads or in-app purchases? No.

Which languages are supported? English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German. The game follows your device language.

How do I turn the sound off? Tap ⏸ at the top right during a round, then switch sound on or off on the pause screen.
