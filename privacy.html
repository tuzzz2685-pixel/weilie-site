《蛇宝开饭啦》隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日　·　开发者：Yufei Wang

《蛇宝开饭啦》是一款单机休闲游戏，完全在你的设备上运行。

我们不收集任何数据

游戏不需要注册账号，不联网，不展示广告，不做任何追踪或统计分析，也不会把任何信息发送到我们或第三方的服务器。我们不收集你的姓名、联系方式、位置、设备标识或游戏记录。

保存在你设备上的内容

最高分和音效开关保存在你的设备本地，只供游戏自己读取，不会上传。删除 App 即可清除。

儿童

本游戏不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

政策变更与联系我们

Snack Snake (蛇宝开饭啦) — Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026 · Developer: Yufei Wang

Snack Snake (蛇宝开饭啦) is a single-player casual game that runs entirely on your device.

We collect no data

The game has no accounts, makes no network connections, shows no ads, and does no tracking or analytics. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. We do not collect your name, contact details, location, device identifiers or game history.

On your device

Your best score and sound setting are stored only on your device, for the game's own use. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If a future version adds online features, this page will be updated, with a new effective date, before that version is released. Questions: wangtuz29@gmail.com.
